About

Soil Scientist at the Plant–Water Nexus

I am a postdoctoral researcher and soil scientist with expertise in soil organic matter fractionation, solid-state 13C NMR spectroscopy, DRIFTS, and soil function across managed agricultural systems.

My work connects fundamental mechanisms of carbon persistence and transformation with field-scale management questions, especially where soil carbon, nutrient cycling, and water-related functions intersect.

I am particularly interested in building mechanistic, management-sensitive indicators that move beyond bulk carbon metrics toward clearer understanding of agroecosystem resilience.

Research

Research Programme

01
Soil Organic Matter Fractionation and Persistence
Investigating how management reshapes particulate and mineral-associated organic matter pools, and how those shifts relate to carbon persistence and soil function.
POM MAOM NaI Fractionation Soil Carbon
02
Spectroscopy for Mechanistic Soil Understanding
Using solid-state 13C NMR, DRIFTS, and related analytical tools to resolve the molecular nature of soil organic matter and management-sensitive compositional changes.
13C NMR DRIFTS Molecular Composition SOM Chemistry
03
Management, Water Function, and Resilience
Linking management-driven changes in soil organic matter with nutrient and water-related soil functions, with the goal of identifying meaningful indicators of resilience.
Water Function Resilience Grazing Agricultural Soils
